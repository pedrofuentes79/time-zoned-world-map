Correction to my comment above — I had the mechanism wrong.

I said Trindade and Martim Vaz sit in no timezone relation. They aren't in a boundary=timezone relation, but they are covered by relation 54882 (Espirito Santo), which carries timezone=America/Sao_Paulo and has the islands' territorial-sea rings (ways 770537922 and 770537923) among its members. Since the builder queries relation["timezone"="<tzid>"] without filtering on boundary=timezone, that's what puts the islands in UTC-3. That also makes your revert rationale here read differently to me than it did when I wrote the above — it's a data-model point, not a legal one, and on that level it's correct.

It follows that adding those rings to this relation on its own would just create an America/Noronha vs America/Sao_Paulo overlap, with nothing in expectedZoneOverlaps.json to cover it. So I'm not going to make that edit unilaterally. Please also disregard the way IDs I listed — those were coastline ways, which would have been the wrong things to use here regardless.

The legal point I think still stands: Lei 12.876/2013 places Fernando de Noronha, Atol das Rocas, and Trindade/Martim Vaz in UTC-2 regardless of which state administers them, and zone1970.tab describes America/Noronha as "Atlantic islands" in the plural. But actually fixing it looks like it needs both halves — the relation membership plus a difference op in timezones.json so America/Sao_Paulo subtracts America/Noronha. I'll open an issue on the repo with the details rather than keep this on a changeset thread.
